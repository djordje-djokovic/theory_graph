My co-founder, Dr. Fatma Kaplan, has been studying pheromones for more than 10 years. She discovered the first C. elegans sex pheromone. This is pretty amazing, considering C. elegans is a hermaphrodite. Then she found other behaviors that were also controlled by pheromones, This gives us the power to control nematodes. One such behavior is dispersal. In the absence of pheromones, nematodes stay put and don't move around very much. But when you add the pheromones, they disperse and they look for new food. We've taken this technology and used it to create our first two products, Ferrocote and Nemastim. Ferrocote is a seed treatment that is applied to seeds before they are planted, and it protects the young and vulnerable roots from attack by plant parasitic nematodes.

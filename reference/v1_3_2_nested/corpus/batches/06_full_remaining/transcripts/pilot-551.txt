As the old saying goes, it's the eye of the master that fattens the cattle. In Portuguese, we say, olho do dono. When managing a livestock farm, to monitor the weight of the cattle is crucial. Weight indicates the cow's health, genetic evolution, right time to sell, and the financial value of livestock. To summarize it, more weight, more money. Currently, obtaining weight information is labor-intensive, time-consuming, and expensive, so it's rarely obtained. Worse, weaning the cow is stressful for the animal, can lead to injuries, and the cow loses weight. Less weight, less money. Olho do Dono provides unprecedented insight into livestock management. Our proprietary software uses a portable 3D camera to estimate the weight of the cattle, allowing farmers to monitor weight variation frequently at a stress-free manner for the cow, at a rate that is 10 times faster than the traditional process. With access to this data, farmers know when to sell, change feed and supplement, treat sick cows, prescribe the right amount of medicine, identify the best genetics for the next purchase.
